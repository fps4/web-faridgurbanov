title: Learning
summary: Video series on building and running AI products and the software around them, from an engineer who builds the data and integration platforms these systems depend on.
---

Each series is a set of short episodes, five or six minutes each. An episode takes one question,
shows a real case, and ends on one card: the idea, the move, and what to do on Monday. Episodes come
out one at a time as I record them.
