summary: Free video courses on data, integration and AI platforms, from an engineer who designs and builds them.

Each course is a set of short episodes, and some come with labs you run yourself. An episode takes
one design question and explains the reasoning: what the choice costs, what goes wrong in real
estates, and what to ask the team that runs the platform. Episodes come out one at a time as I
record them.
