## What this is

Six modules of short episodes, five or six minutes each. Every episode takes one question, shows a
real case from the research or the news, says what the research found, and works it through on one
fictional company. Where I have seen it go wrong in my own projects, I say so.

Each module stands alone. Start with the one you need.

## Who it is for

- **Engineers and architects.** You are asked to build or connect an AI system, and want the
  questions to ask before the first sprint.
- **Product owners and data leads.** You own the result, and want to know what to measure and who
  signs off.
- **Managers who fund it.** You want to know what a credible plan contains, and what it costs to
  keep running.

## One company, six cases

Werkmora is a home, DIY and garden retailer in the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany. Each module
takes a different use case in the same company, with enough setup to follow it on its own.

- **175** stores in three countries.
- **€3.4 billion** a year in sales.
- **13,500** people.
- **15%** of sales online.

Werkmora is fictional. It is built from public facts about real retailers, and its numbers are
rounded and made up. The cases and research around it are real, with their sources.
