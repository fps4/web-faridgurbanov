---
title: AI Product Design
summary: From a costly decision to an AI product people trust. Eight parts, published one at a time, on one running example.
---

Two years into AI, most organizations look the same. Nearly everyone has a personal copilot, one team
experiments with something more ambitious, and no use case runs across the organization, built or
bought. This storyline walks through how an AI product gets decided and built, one step per part, and
ends every part in your organization: the move, who makes it, how you will know it worked, and a
download for next week's meeting.

The steps follow the field's own frameworks, and each part names the one it uses. Every part shows its
step on the same running example: a mid-size insurer where a claims handler reads every small
home-contents claim by hand, and wants the clear ones paid the same day. The insurer is fictional and
its numbers are synthetic; the facts about the insurance market around it are real, with their
sources.

A part is a page, two short episodes (slides, with me in the corner), and the downloads: a canvas, a
scorecard, a register or a plan, blank and filled in for the example. Part 8 is about building it
with coding agents, and about who signs.
